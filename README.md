## My Favorite Movie: Josee, the Tiger and the Fish

**Genre:** Romance, Drama  
**Director:** Kotaro Tamura  

### Summary
The story follows Tsuneo, a college student, who meets Josee, a young woman who uses a wheelchair and lives a sheltered life. Their friendship grows as they help each other face dreams and fears.

### Why I Like It
- Beautiful animation  
- Strong emotional story  
- Inspiring message about independence and love
